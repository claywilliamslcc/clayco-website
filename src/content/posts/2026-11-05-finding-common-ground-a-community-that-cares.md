Welcome to Common Ground Community Nonprofit!  We provide an inclusive, judgement free space where all students feel supported and empowered to prioritize their health. Check out our accessible resources, including:

* Individual and group counseling.
* Mental health and wellness workshops.
* Peer support and substance abuse counseling. 
* A free thrift store, food pantry, and personal hygiene products for participants.
* Sexual and reproductive health resources, including STI testing events, free pregnancy tests, and condoms.

Our mission is to promote college students' well-being through self care education, burnout prevention, substance use support, peer connection, and access to sexual and reproductive health resources and counseling.
